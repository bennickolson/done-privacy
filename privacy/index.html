Privacy Policy

Your routines stay on your device. You decide what to export or send.

Effective date: 2026-11-13
Operated by: Benjamin Nickolson
Privacy and support: ben.nickolson@gmail.com

Your routines and settings

DONE. lets you track routines without creating an account. Routine names, icons, schedules, check-ins, progress history, archive information, daily targets and preferences are stored in the app’s storage on your device. DONE. also keeps local recovery copies and may preserve original records during restoration. These are not an online account or a cloud-sync service.

Daily reminders

If you enable Daily Reminder, DONE. asks for notification permission and uses iOS local notifications. Reminder intent, time, scheduling identifiers, dates and recent scheduling history are stored locally. The notification says “Your daily rhythm is ready.” and does not include routine names. No push-notification server is used for this feature.

You can turn reminders off in DONE. and control notification permission in iOS Settings. DONE. queues eligible days for a limited period, up to 28 calendar days. Open the app periodically to refresh future scheduling. iOS controls whether and when notifications appear.

Backups and restoration

You can export a JSON backup and choose where to save it. Backups are not encrypted by DONE. They can contain routine names, schedules, check-ins, archive history, daily targets, export timestamps, and celebration, haptics, progress-display and accent settings. Keep them private. Reminder settings and the separate appearance-mode preference are not included in this habit-backup format.

Importing reads the file you select and shows a preview before you confirm replacement. Automatic recovery copies remain separate from exported files. Raw recovery exports can contain original, unreadable or older records and should also be treated as private.

Depending on your device settings, Apple device backups may include app data. Apple controls these services; they are not a DONE. cloud service. See Apple’s privacy information.

Progress sharing

Progress images are generated on your device. Images can reveal dates, progress, optional titles, routine names and statistics depending on your choices. Review the preview before sharing or copying. If you choose to share an image or backup, the selected person, app or storage provider receives that content and handles it under its own policies. Copying an image places it on your clipboard, which is controlled by your device and its settings. DONE. does not upload habit records to a developer-operated sharing service.

Libraries and diagnostics

This release does not include developer analytics, advertising tracking or an account service receiving your habit records. It uses Capacitor libraries to connect local storage, reminders, preferences, app lifecycle and haptic feedback with iOS. Local errors and technical console messages may be produced for troubleshooting; DONE. does not include a developer-operated automatic error-upload service.

Apple may process app/device diagnostics according to your Apple settings and services, and may make diagnostics and feedback available to developers through its developer services. This is separate from local habit storage.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to include. Email uses your provider and ours. Please avoid sending private routines, complete backups or diagnostics unless necessary and separately agreed.

Support enquiries are received through a dedicated, monitored email account and reviewed manually by the app developer. Emails may contain your email address, message and attachments you voluntarily provide, such as screenshots or diagnostic information. This information is used solely to respond to support requests, investigate reported issues and resolve problems. It is not sold or used for advertising. Emails and attachments are stored securely within the email provider’s service, accessible only to authorised personnel, and deleted when no longer reasonably required for support, subject to applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords or other sensitive personal information.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete Data requires explicit confirmation. A successfully completed action removes the active habit record, local recovery snapshots, preserved originals, legacy DONE. browser records and app-owned temporary backup-export files. It resets the habit-backed settings. A small deletion-completion marker remains to prevent old records being recovered automatically.

This action is not a complete reset of every device preference. The separate reminder preference record, including its time, enabled intent and scheduling history, is not erased by that action. DONE. attempts to cancel its owned pending reminders when routine data becomes unavailable or is empty; this is separate from verified habit-file deletion, and failures may require attention. For control over reminders, turn Daily Reminder off and check iOS notification settings. Do not interpret Delete Data as proof that all system-delivered notifications or every preference has been removed.

Exported files, saved or shared images, clipboard content, recipients’ copies, support emails and device backups are outside this action. Manage those separately with their providers. Deleting a single routine is different from Delete Data: recovery copies may retain earlier records. Removing the app can remove its local data; offloading is not the same as deleting its data. DONE. cannot recover records after all accessible copies are removed.

This website

Reading these pages does not give us access to the app’s local habit records. The pages include no analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, external fonts or CDN scripts.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. These hosting records are separate from your local habit records.

Changes and contact

Policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions, questions about information sent to support, or help with DONE., contact ben.nickolson@gmail.com or visit Support.
