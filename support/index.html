A reminder didn’t appear

  1. Open DONE. and check Settings > Daily Reminder is enabled and its local time is correct. Make sure at least one active routine is scheduled for the day.
  2. In iPhone Settings > Notifications > DONE., check notification permission and presentation options. Check Focus and Scheduled Summary, which may silence or delay presentation.
  3. Check the iPhone’s date and time zone are correct. Reopen DONE. after travel or changing reminder settings. Do not change the clock just to force a reminder.
  4. Open DONE. periodically. It schedules a limited queue of eligible days, up to 28 calendar days, and cannot refresh indefinitely while closed. Capacity limits or errors shown in the app can also affect future reminders.

The body is “Your daily rhythm is ready.” without routine names. The app avoids repeating a reminder already consumed for the same day; changing the time does not promise another notification that day. iOS controls delivery. Turn Daily Reminder off to cancel owned pending reminders; if the app reports an error, check it rather than assuming cancellation succeeded. Notification permission is controlled separately in iOS Settings.

Keep a backup outside the app

In Settings > Export & Backup, export a file and choose its destination. Keep a private copy somewhere you can access if the device is lost. Automatic local recovery copies are on the same device and are not a replacement for this. JSON backups are not encrypted by DONE. and can contain routine names and history. Your chosen storage provider may make its own copies.

Habit backups include routines, check-ins, archive information, daily targets and celebration, haptics, progress-display and accent settings. They do not include reminder settings or the separate appearance-mode preference. Check those settings after moving to another device.

Restore or recover without losing originals

Keep your existing exported files. Use Export & Backup to select a known backup, review the preview and confirm only if you want to replace the current records. If possible, export the current state first.

If DONE. shows a saving or recovery warning, stop making changes and read it. Do not uninstall, reset, clear app storage or delete data as an initial troubleshooting step. Export the latest in-memory data or raw recovery records if the app offers that option, keep originals private, and contact support with the warning text. A recovery copy may be available for review; do not overwrite it blindly. Raw recovery files are not ordinary importable backups.

Share only what you intend

Review the image preview, optional title, routine name, dates and statistics before using Share / Save or Copy. The selected recipient, storage service or clipboard receives your chosen content. Removing a local routine later does not remove those copies.

Understand Delete Data

Settings > Delete Data is destructive and requires confirmation. Successful completion removes habit records, local recovery snapshots, preserved originals, legacy DONE. browser records and app-owned temporary backup-export files. A deletion-completion marker remains to prevent automatic recovery. Deleting one routine does not erase every older recovery copy.

Delete Data does not erase the separate reminder preference record. Turn Daily Reminder off and use iOS Settings to control permission. Cancellation of owned pending reminders is a separate best-effort process; follow any error messages. Previously delivered notifications may still need to be cleared in iOS.

Files you saved elsewhere, shared images, clipboard content, emails, recipients’ copies and Apple device backups must be managed separately. Offloading an app is not the same as deleting its data. Once every accessible copy is gone, DONE. cannot recover it.
